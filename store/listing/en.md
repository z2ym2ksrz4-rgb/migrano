## Description
Migrano is the migraine diary you can actually keep — one tap when an attack starts, the details when you feel better, and a PDF your neurologist reads in a minute.

ONE TAP WHEN IT STARTS
"Attack now" starts the clock. Intensity, triggers, symptoms and medication can wait. End it when it's over, or let the reminder ask you 24 hours later.

MADE FOR THE DOCTOR'S VISIT
• Attack days and medication days per month — the two numbers every neurologist asks for
• Calendar heatmap of your month
• Insights: average duration and intensity, which triggers and symptoms come up most
• PDF report for any period, one row per attack
• Medication-day warning at the limit you choose (10 or 15 days) — Migrano never diagnoses, it just tells you when it's worth talking to your doctor

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No cloud. No analytics. Everything stays on your phone, protected by a PIN if you want, and you can export an encrypted backup any time.

FREE AND PRO
Logging, check-ins, calendar and medication days are free, forever. Migrano Pro adds insights, the PDF report, the medication-day warning, PIN lock, backup and unlimited history — yearly with a 7-day free trial, or one lifetime purchase. No ads, ever.

Migrano is a self-observation diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment. Talk to your doctor about what you log.
